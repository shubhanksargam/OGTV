About

GTV

YouTube, the way TV used to be

OGTV turns Indian YouTube into 33 always-on channels, from cricket and live news to Bhakti, Bollywood hits and science. No searching, no feeds: switch on, flip channels, watch what's on.

Everyone sees the same moment

Each channel runs on a schedule built from time-of-day rules, so every viewer tuned to a channel is at the same point of the same programme, like real TV. New uploads from 160+ creators, channels and playlists are added every night, and live channels pick up official streams as they go on air.
