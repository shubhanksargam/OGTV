Privacy Policy

Version 1.1, in force from 22 November 2026.

OGTV keeps as little about you as it can. There are no accounts: you watch without signing up, and OGTV stores nothing that identifies you.

1. Who runs OGTV

OGTV (ogtv.co.in) is made and run by Shubhank Sargam, in India. For any question or request about privacy, write to privacy@ogtv.co.in.

2. What is collected, and why

WhatWhy
Your IP address and browser details reach Cloudflare, which delivers the site, as with any website. OGTV keeps none of it.To deliver the pages and protect them from attacks
A count of visits by page, and of events such as a channel being watched or a video failing to play, with no cookies and no stored IP addresses (GoatCounter)To know, in total, which channels people watch and which videos don't play
Settings kept only in your browser: volume, Retro and Radio mode, the channel you were on, and videos that wouldn't play on your deviceTo remember your choices. OGTV never receives them.

OGTV does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, your location or your contacts, keeps no record of what you personally watch (only totals, as above), does not use data for advertising, and never sells it.

3. YouTube

Every video plays in YouTube's own player, embedded from YouTube. While it plays, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. OGTV uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service. OGTV does not receive what YouTube collects.

4. OGTV's YouTube channel and its playlist tool

OGTV has its own YouTube channel, @ogtvindia. A small tool run by OGTV keeps that channel's public playlists up to date through YouTube API Services. It signs in only to OGTV's own YouTube channel, with OGTV's permission, and only creates and updates OGTV's own playlists. It does not access, collect, store or share any viewer's Google or YouTube data, and viewers never sign in to it.

Its sign-in is kept only as an encrypted secret in OGTV's build system (GitHub Actions), never on the website. The permission can be withdrawn at any time at Google's security settings. OGTV's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Who else handles data

These services may process data outside India, including in the United States and the EU. OGTV shares information with authorities only when the law requires it.

6. Cookies

OGTV sets no cookies. YouTube's player may set its own, and Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie.

7. Children

OGTV is for a general audience, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included.

8. Your rights

Because OGTV holds no personal data about you, there is nothing of yours to access, correct or delete. Clearing your browser's data for ogtv.co.in removes the settings kept on your device. For any question, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, write to privacy@ogtv.co.in.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date.
